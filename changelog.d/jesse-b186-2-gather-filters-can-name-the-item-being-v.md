A gather filter or a tile's visibility can now name the item the page is showing it beside (B186): two new contextual values, `current_item` (the item's id) and `current_item_field` (one of its fields), resolve from whichever item the page renders, so one "other articles" or "related by tag" tile placed once in a region serves every item page rather than needing a copy per item. A tile's visibility also gained an `item_types` rule, restricting it to pages showing an item of the given content types. On a page showing no item, a gather naming `current_item` returns nothing rather than every row, and a gather result built from the current item is never reused across different items.
