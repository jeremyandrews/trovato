Search analytics: what visitors search for is counted per UTC day, with the query normalized (lowercased, email addresses and runs of six or more digits replaced) and nothing that identifies a visitor stored, no IP address, account, session, browser or time finer than the day. Counts are kept in memory and flushed every 30 seconds, so recording adds no database work to a search. Server searches are counted on their first page; the browser search page reports its own searches through `navigator.sendBeacon` to `POST /api/search/log`. The expansion cache's hits and misses are counted too. Rows older than the retention (default 90 days) are deleted by cron. The report at `/admin/reports/search` (permission `administer site`) shows totals, the zero result share, the top queries, the top queries that found nothing and the expansion hit rate for 7, 30 or 90 days (BL-111).

**Behavior change:** analytics is on by default on new and existing sites. Switch it off with "Record search analytics" on `/admin/config/search`. The debug log line that printed a raw query on an expansion cache hit is gone.
