## Important notes for successor
- Full cold builds in this pod take 10-20 minutes each (clippy ~12 min,
  doc check ~5 min, unit test compile ~10-20 min just for the kernel
  crate under test profile, final link step is the slow part). Always
  background long cargo invocations and poll every ~5 minutes; do not use
  a single blocking call under ~20 min expecting it to finish, and do not
  assume a timeout means failure.

  `git worktree` checked out at a different commit -- it corrupts cached
  artifacts for crates whose source differs between the two checkouts
